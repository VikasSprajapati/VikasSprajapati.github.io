Case study 03 · Freelance product

A field-force HRMS, from kickoff to production in two months.

Zob Connect tracks attendance, onboarding and compliance for staff who work at retail sites: a REST API, a React web console, and React Native apps for Android and iOS. I led a team of four and owned the architecture, the integrations and the release.

The problem

Field staff work at retail sites, not in an office. The client needed to know who actually showed up, where and when, and to onboard new staff with verified identity and documents before their first shift, not weeks after it.

It had to work on a phone in a shop, for people who speak many languages, and give managers a register they could trust.

What I built

A mobile app for the field, and a console for HR and managers.

Attendance

Attendance a manager can check

Every punch records where it came from and how far that is from the site.

  • Selfie attendance with face verification, checked against the site's geofence
  • Punches outside the fence are saved and flagged for a manager, never refused
  • Shift-aware clock-in and clock-out rules, and a monthly log of every day
  • Corrections keep the original punch beside them
Onboarding

Onboarding with KYC built in

A new employee is verified and documented before they can use the app.

  • PAN, Aadhaar and bank verification APIs, with a gate that blocks the app until verified
  • An audited document register on Google Drive (OAuth 2.0), with access logs and masked ID numbers
  • Bulk employee and target import
  • Role-based access for HR, zone, site and team managers
Reports

Reports for every level

Attendance, sales and deployment, all following the same filters, and exportable.

  • Filter by period, zone, state, city, store, team lead or designation
  • Attendance rate, absences, late arrivals, out-of-fence punches, average hours
  • Every figure on screen can be exported

The mobile app

React Native with Expo, for Android and iOS. These screens show my own test account.

  • Live capture: each mark is a photo stamped with the time and location; gallery uploads are not accepted
  • Regularise: missed a punch? Raise a correction from the phone
  • 10 Indian languages
  • Over-the-air updates with Expo EAS, so fixes reach phones without a reinstall

How we delivered it

Requirements with the client

Workflows, roles and compliance rules agreed with the client up front.

AI-first build

Built with Claude Code for implementation, code review, tests, debugging and translation, all reviewed by the team.

Launch and stabilise

Production release, then fixes shipped as over-the-air updates, so users never had to reinstall.

What I learned

01
Record, don't refuse

A punch outside the fence is still saved and flagged. Refusing it would cost a worker an hour's pay over a phone glitch; a manager decides instead.

02
Plan for fixes after launch

Over-the-air updates meant the first weeks in production could be fixed quickly, without asking anyone to reinstall.

03
AI speeds up a team; review keeps it safe

Claude Code let four people ship three apps in two months, because every change was still reviewed and tested.
